NOWNEW

NOWNEW is the practice of Christian Heikoop, designer and creative director in Amsterdam. Guided by curiosity rather than product, the work begins with sensing the now, and with the search for what can be done new. Understand­ing comes first, then form, across graphic, product, interior and fashion design, to find the new classics of the here and now.
